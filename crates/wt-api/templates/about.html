About

Wheel Turning helps you find what Zen teachers and foundational texts have said about the questions that come up in practice. Every answer is made of short excerpts, each with its source, and the site writes none of the excerpts itself.

What this is

You describe a question from your practice. The site finds excerpts from recorded talks and from texts such as the Platform Sutra and the Dhammapada. It shows the closest matches first, each under its teacher's name, so you can see how different teachers approach the same difficulty. It does not speak for any teacher.

How answers are chosen

Excerpts are ranked by how well they match your question and by nothing else. Who gave the talk, which sangha it came from, and whether a sangha has a giving page do not change the order. A sangha is a community that practices together. When an answer opens with a summary, a language model writes it from the excerpts below it, and each sentence is checked against the excerpts it cites before you see it. Each number in the summary leads to the exact words it rests on, and the excerpts themselves are always shown in full.

Where the material comes from

Talks come from sangha archives and recordings. Texts come from published editions. The project uses these sources on its own decision, then asks each teacher, sangha and publisher for permission and records the answer. Each excerpt carries a credit line that says exactly what is true: used with permission, used under a named license, or simply where it came from. The page never says a source gave permission unless it did. See every source and its status

Giving

Dana is the Sanskrit and Pali word for generosity. Every excerpt from a talk links to the giving page of the sangha that offered it. After an answer, you can choose to share a gift equally among the sanghas it drew on. This site handles no money and takes no share. The links open each sangha's own page. A separate, optional line is for the site's own running costs, and giving is never needed to use the site.

You choose how your questions are kept.

  • Keep, the default while the test is private: a question is saved for 180 days with obvious personal details removed, such as email addresses, phone numbers, web addresses and long runs of digits. Then its text is deleted and only counts remain.
  • Counts only: a question is saved for 24 hours so you can return to your answer. Then its text is deleted and only counts remain, such as how many teachers and excerpts it drew on, how long it took and clicks on giving links.

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal and you have not chosen, counts only applies. A question that looks like it is about a medical matter or about distress is kept as counts only, whatever you chose. The text of the most serious questions, about harm to yourself or someone else, is never saved. If you flag a question, its text is kept even with counts only, so the project can see what went wrong.

Kept questions can be read at any time by the site's code, its owner and approved team members, through database queries or a simple dashboard. The project reads them to learn which questions the collection cannot answer yet. Feedback notes are read too, but the reports that show them never show your question with them unless you flagged it.

You can change your choice or delete all your questions at any time. Deleting removes every question you asked here, with its answers, your feedback and your clicks on giving links.

Your question, and the excerpts found for it, are sent to a language model provider to check the question for sensitive topics, to check which excerpts fit it and where to cut them, and to write the summary. Under its current terms, the provider does not train on what it receives and deletes it after no more than 30 days. It may keep a request longer if it flags it for a misuse review. The provider can change, and this page will say so if its terms do.

The site reaches you through Cloudflare, which protects it from abuse. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives each request, your questions included, and your IP address, so that it can deliver the page. It handles them under its own privacy policy. We do not use Cloudflare to identify you.

Student voices from recorded classes are never stored. When a teacher answers a student's question, the question is shown before the answer in a lightly edited form, with names and identifying details removed, and labeled "Student". To make that edit, the student's question is sent to the same provider under the same terms, and only the edited text is kept.

For any other privacy request, such as asking what we hold about you, write to hello@wheelturning.org.
