About

Wheel Turning helps you find what Zen teachers have said about the questions that come up in practice. Every answer is made of short excerpts, each with its source, and the site writes none of the excerpts itself.

How answers are chosen

Excerpts are ranked by how well they match your question and by nothing else. Who gave the talk, which sangha it came from, and whether a sangha has a giving page do not change the order. A sangha is a community that practices together.

Your questions

Your questions are logged. They are read only in aggregate, and one is opened only if you flag it. Student questions and student voices from recorded classes are never stored.
