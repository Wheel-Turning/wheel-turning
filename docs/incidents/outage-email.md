# Email to testers about an outage

Runbook procedure 13, steps 6 and 9. Send from the owner's address with every tester in BCC,
never To or CC. Fill in the brackets. Leave the cause out while it is unknown or touches
security, and never name the language model provider.

## When it starts

**Subject:** Wheel Turning is down for now

Hello,

Wheel Turning has been unavailable since [time and time zone], because [a short plain cause, or
"of a problem I am still looking into"]. Your invite link still works, and nothing you asked or
sent is lost.

[Only if tester data may be affected: what, plainly, and what is being done about it.]

I will write again when it is back. You do not need to do anything.

Thank you for your patience,
[name]

## When it is resolved

**Subject:** Wheel Turning is back

Hello,

Wheel Turning is working again as of [time and time zone]. [One sentence on what happened and
what changed so it does not happen again.]

Your invite link works as before. If anything looks wrong, reply to this email or use the
feedback form under any answer.

Thank you,
[name]
