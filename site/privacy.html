Privacy

Last updated October 7, 2026

This website sets no cookies and asks you for nothing. We count visits with a cookie-free analytics tool and see only totals. Beyond that, we receive only what you choose to send us by email.

Who we are

Wheel Turning is a free, noncommercial project based in Washington State, run by an individual for now, with a plan to form a nonprofit. In this policy, "we" means the person who runs the project. You can reach us at hello@wheelturning.org.

What this website collects

This website is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no advertising and no tracking pixels. The only script it loads is the analytics script described below.

Visit counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to learn how many people visit and which pages they read. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not follow you to other websites. It records details such as the page visited, the site that linked to it, the browser and device type and the country, and we see these only as totals. We never try to identify individual visitors.

Our web host

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives the technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, so that it can deliver the page and protect the site from abuse. Cloudflare handles that information under its own privacy policy. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not combine it with anything else.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your message and anything else you include. We use it only to read and answer your message and to act on what you ask.

  • If you ask us to remove material, we keep a record of the request, what was removed and when. We keep that record so the same material is not added again.
  • If you give or limit permission, we keep a record of your answer, because the site's credit lines must say exactly what is true.
  • Other messages are kept only as long as they are needed to answer you.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and we never use it for advertising. Email to our address is forwarded to the project's private mailbox, so the mail providers involved also process it to deliver it.

The private test

The search tool itself is in a private test with invited people only. Testers are told how their questions are handled before they start, and they can switch off keeping their questions or delete them at any time. Questions and voices from students in recorded classes are never stored. When the tool opens more widely, this page will be updated to describe it in full before anyone else can use it.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hello@wheelturning.org. We will answer, and we will not ask why. A removal record described above is kept even after the rest is deleted, because deleting it would let removed material return.

Health information

This website does not collect health information. If you choose to mention your health in an email, we use it only to answer you and do not share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
