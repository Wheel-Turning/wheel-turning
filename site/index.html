What it will be

  • Passages, not paraphrase

    You describe a question from your practice. The site shows short excerpts from recorded talks and foundational texts, each linked to the moment or page it came from. The site writes none of the excerpts itself.

  • Teachers in their own voices

    Excerpts are grouped by teacher, so you can see how different teachers approach the same difficulty. The site does not speak for any teacher.

  • Relevance alone ranks

    Excerpts are ordered by how well they match your question and by nothing else. No payment, partnership or upload volume changes the order.

  • Gifts go to sanghas

    Dana is the Sanskrit and Pali word for generosity. Each excerpt from a talk links to its sangha's own giving page. The site handles no money and takes no share.

Get in touch

Wheel Turning is free and noncommercial, with no advertising and no fees.

We add talks and texts only where, in our judgement, the rights holder's terms allow it, explicitly or implicitly. We ask every rights holder for permission. Where the rights are unclear, we ask first and add nothing until they say yes. Where the terms are more permissive, as with foundational texts, we add the material and remove it at once if they say no.

If you are a teacher, sangha or publisher, you can give permission, set limits or have anything removed. See Rights and removal, or write to hello@wheelturning.org.
