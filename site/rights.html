Rights and removal

If you hold rights in any talk or text Wheel Turning uses, you can have it removed. Tell us and we will remove it, without argument.

How it works

  1. Within one business day of your request, the material you name stops appearing. We do this before checking who you are.
  2. Then we delete the text and our stored copies, and we block the same material from being added again, even from a different web address.
  3. If it turns out you do not hold the rights, we may restore it, and we will tell you first.
  4. If you would rather give permission or set limits, such as leaving out certain talks or topics, tell us that instead. We will record it and follow it.

Teachers

You can choose less than full removal at any time with one message: excerpts only, nothing shown publicly, or a copy of your transcripts for your own use. You can also say yes in writing, and the pages will then say your talks are used with your permission.

Student voices

Questions and voices from students in recorded classes are never stored or shown.

How to reach us

Write to rights@wheelturning.org, or reply to any letter you received from the project. Tell us what should be removed or limited: a web address, a title, or the code shown next to an excerpt. Your name is optional, but we can only reply if you give a way to reach you.

Replies may take a few days. We act on the request first and reply after.

Sources and their status

When the tool opens beyond its private test, this page will list every source and its status in plain words: used on the project's own decision with the date permission was requested, used with permission, used under a named license, or removed at the rights holder's request.
