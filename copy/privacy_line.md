Unless you choose counts only, your questions are kept for 180 days with obvious personal details, such as email addresses and phone numbers, removed. Kept questions can be read at any time by the site code, the owner and approved team members. Questions are also sent to a language model provider to check them and to find and summarize excerpts. With counts only, the text of a question is deleted after 24 hours and only counts remain. You can delete all your questions at any time.
